## Team checklist
- [ ] Team of the size the rules allow, with mixed skills (backend, frontend, cloud, design, presenting)
- [ ] Clear roles: who builds, who tests, who pitches
- [ ] Shared GitHub repo from day one
- [ ] One-paragraph problem statement
- [ ] Simple architecture diagram
- [ ] Working demo before polish
- [ ] Budget alert set; resources deleted afterwards
- [ ] No secrets in the repo
- [ ] 3-minute pitch practised at least twice

## Pitch structure
1. **Problem:** who has it and why it matters
2. **Solution:** what you built, in one sentence
3. **Demo:** show it working
4. **How it works:** architecture in 30 seconds
5. **Impact and next steps**
