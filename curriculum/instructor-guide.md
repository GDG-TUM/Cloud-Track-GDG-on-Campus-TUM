# Instructor and Facilitator Guide

[← Curriculum overview](README.md)

## Before the session
- Read the weekly guide and complete the lab from a clean environment.
- Verify links, commands, current product interfaces, quotas and pricing.
- Prepare a working demo and fallback recording or screenshots.
- Identify the core outcome and one optional extension.
- Ensure the exercise does not expose credentials or personal data.
- Provide a local or low-bandwidth alternative where practical.
- Prepare a cleanup checklist for cloud resources.

## 90-minute session pattern
| Time | Activity |
|---:|---|
| 0–10 min | Welcome, recap, goals and prerequisite check |
| 10–25 min | Explain the mental model and vocabulary |
| 25–40 min | Instructor demonstration, including a debugging example |
| 40–70 min | Guided hands-on build |
| 70–82 min | Checkpoint, peer explanation and extension |
| 82–90 min | Cleanup, reflection and next-week preparation |

## Mixed-ability teaching
- Start with a visible outcome and explain unfamiliar terms.
- Demonstrate commands in small steps; explain flags and expected output.
- Give beginners a working baseline, checklist and known-good starting point.
- Give intermediate learners extension tasks involving a decision, comparison or diagnosis.
- Pair learners intentionally and rotate who drives the keyboard.
- Ask learners to explain results in their own words.
- Normalize debugging; demonstrate how to inspect errors.

## Accessibility
Share materials before sessions; use readable diagrams, descriptive links and copyable commands; do not rely only on colour; explain acronyms; allow equivalent evidence when device, connectivity or account constraints prevent a cloud lab; keep exercises pausable; do not ask members to disclose personal or financial information.

## Safety and cost
Never ask members to share credentials. Prefer short-lived, scoped credentials. A budget notification is not a spending cap. Avoid expensive or always-on services. Show how to inspect and delete temporary infrastructure. Explain potential charges and alternatives before enabling billing. Use synthetic data.

## After the session
Publish corrected notes and links, record common errors and fixes, review checkpoint feedback, identify members needing support, adapt the next session if prerequisites were missed, and confirm temporary resources were removed.
