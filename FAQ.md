# ❓ FAQ

### Do I need experience to join?
No. The track starts from the basics. If you can use a laptop and want to learn, you are welcome.

### Is it free?
The sessions and resources are free. Cloud providers can charge for resources left running, so we teach cost control in Week 1 and use free-tier or trial offers where available. Always check the provider's current terms.

### What if I miss a session?
Everything is in this repo. Open the week's folder, read the README, do the challenge, and ask questions in the track channel.

### My laptop is old or slow. Can I still take part?
Yes. Use **Cloud Shell** (a free browser-based terminal in Google Cloud) and GitHub Codespaces if available to you. Tell the Track Lead and we will help you find a setup.

### Why Google Cloud?
We are a Google Developer Group chapter, so we focus there. The ideas (compute, storage, networking, identity, CI/CD, IaC, security) transfer directly to AWS and Azure.

### Can I learn AWS or Azure here too?
Yes, as optional extras. Share what you learn in a pull request to the `resources` folder.

### How do I get help?
Open a question issue, ask in the track channel, or message the Track Lead.

### How do I contribute?
Start with [adding your profile](members/README.md), then read the org [CONTRIBUTING guide](https://github.com/GDG-TUM/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

### I accidentally committed a secret. What now?
Tell a maintainer **immediately**. The secret must be revoked and replaced, since deleting the commit is not enough. See the [security policy](https://github.com/GDG-TUM/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

### Do I get a certificate?
Active members who complete the challenges and a capstone get recognised at demo day. For industry certifications, see [certifications](resources/certifications.md).
