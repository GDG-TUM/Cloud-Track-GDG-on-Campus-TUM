## Phases

1. **Foundations (weeks 1-2):** concepts, accounts, tools
2. **Build (weeks 3-6):** deploy, containerize, explore AI, hackathon
3. **Ship and secure (weeks 7-9):** automation, IaC, security
4. **Show (week 10):** demo day and retrospective

## Principles
- **Learn by doing:** every session ends with something working
- **Small steps:** challenges take 1 to 2 hours
- **Public by default:** work goes on GitHub
- **Cost-aware and secure from day one**
- **Help each other:** teach what you just learned
